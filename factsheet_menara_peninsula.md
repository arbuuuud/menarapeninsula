- **Official Website**: https://www.menarapeninsula.com/

- **Positioning**: "Where Professionalism Meets Luxury, Every Stay Matters" / "The Crown Jewel of West Jakarta".

  - Sudirman Central Business District (SCBD): ~20 minutes drive.

## 2. Check-In & Check-Out Policies
- **Check-In Time**: From 14:00 (2:00 PM) onwards.
- **Check-Out Time**: By 12:00 (12:00 PM) noon.
- **Security Deposit**: 
  - A refundable damage deposit of **IDR 500,000** (or IDR 400,000 depending on booking terms) is required upon arrival (cash or credit card authorization). 
  - Fully refunded upon check-out if no damage or incidental losses are found.
- **Reception / Front Desk**: 24/7 Operations.

## 3. General Amenities & Property Facilities
- **WiFi**: Free high-speed wireless internet access across all rooms and public spaces.
- **Recreation**:
  - **Outdoor Swimming Pool**: Covered/semi-outdoor with glass ceiling, including a children's pool.
  - **Fitness Center / Gym**: Fully equipped health club.
  - **Artha Spa & Massage**: Aromatherapies and full-body massages. Open daily from 08:00 to 23:00.
  - **Sauna & Whirlpool**: Accessible via the health club.
- **Services**:
  - 24-Hour Room Service
  - 24-Hour Front Office & Duty Manager
  - Concierge & Bell Desk services
  - Luggage Storage
  - Laundry & Dry Cleaning services
  - Car Rental and Transportation desk
  - Business Center (with secretarial/printing services)
  - Security: 24-hour security patrols and CCTV surveillance throughout the property.
  - Parking: Onsite secure parking space accommodating up to 200 cars.
  - Accessibility: Wheelchairs available; fire alarm and safety systems in place.

## 4. Food & Beverage Outlets
- **Kafe Coleman**: 
  - All-day dining restaurant serving international, Asian, and traditional Indonesian specialties (Halal menu).
  - **Location**: Lobby Level.
  - **Hours**: Open daily from 06:00 to 23:00. (Breakfast buffet: 06:00 – 10:00, Lunch: 12:00 – 14:30, Dinner: 18:00 – 21:00).
  - **Seating Capacity**: 170 persons.
- **Symphony Lounge**:
  - A chic and elegant lounge offering Segafredo Coffee, a premium selection of teas, drinks, and an extensive light bites menu.
  - **Location**: Mezzanine Level.
  - **Hours**: Open daily from 09:00 to 24:00 (Midnight).
  - **Seating Capacity**: 60 persons.
- **Alfresco Private Dining Venue**:
  - Poolside semi-outdoor outlet perfect for private events, birthday parties, anniversaries, and social gatherings with panoramic city skyline views.
  - **Location**: 5th Level.
  - **Hours**: Flexible based on events.
  - **Seating Capacity**: 80 persons (indoors) and up to 200 persons poolside (outdoors).
- **The Executive Lounge**:
  - Private lounge offering exclusive privileges for Executive Club guests (priority check-in/out, buffet breakfast, afternoon tea, evening cocktails).
  - **Location**: 23rd Floor.

## 5. Child & Extra Bed Policies
- **Child Policy**:
  - Children between the ages of 2 to 5 stay free of charge when using existing bedding.
  - Baby cots are available free of charge upon request (subject to availability).
  - Buffet breakfast charge for children: IDR 90,000 net (approximate) / Adult breakfast charge: IDR 180,000 net per person.
- **Extra Bed Policy**:
  - **Extra Bed Price**: **IDR 350,000** net per person, per night (includes breakfast, subject to request and room type availability).
  - Maximum extra beds allowed in a room: 1 (applicable only to select configurations like Deluxe, Studios, and Suites; not applicable to rooms with double twin setups if space is restricted).

---

## 6. Airport Transfer Services
- **Airport Transfer Fee**: **IDR 380,000** net per car, per way.
- **Details**: Operates 24 hours (advance reservation required with flight details). Standard service handles route between Soekarno-Hatta International Airport and Menara Peninsula Hotel.

---

## 7. Cancellation & Prepayment Policies
- **Standard Policy**: Cancellation and prepayment terms vary depending on the selected room type, rate plan, and reservation channel.
- **Flexible Rates**: Cancellations made up to 24 or 48 hours prior to arrival (depending on rate rules) are free of charge.
- **Non-Refundable / Promotional Rates**: Full prepayment is charged at the time of booking, and no refunds or modifications are allowed.
- **No-Show Penalty**: In case of a no-show under a standard booking, a penalty of 1 night's room charge is applied.

---
