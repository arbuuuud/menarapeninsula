### Pillar 1: Next-Generation Website Development
- **Custom Headless Frontend Engineering**: Built using modern Jamstack technologies (Next.js / Tailwind CSS) ensuring sub-second page loads (<0.8 seconds) and strict adherence to Google Mobile-First indexing.
- **Accommodations Showcase**: Dedicated high-resolution visual showcases for all 8 official room tiers:
  - Deluxe Rooms (28 sqm)
  - Studio Rooms (32 sqm)
  - Junior Suite Rooms (44 sqm)
  - Deluxe Suites Room (44 sqm)
  - Club Rooms (32 sqm)
  - Club Studio Rooms (44 sqm)
  - Club Junior Suites (45 sqm)
  - Club Peninsula Suites (62 sqm)
- **MICE & Weddings Hub**:
  - Comprehensive layout specification display: Classroom (Kunir Room, up to 400 pax), Banquet Gala (Cengkeh Ballroom, 400 pax), Theatre Auditorium (1,200 pax), and Boardroom Executive (Dlima Room, 40–75 pax).
  - Clear exhibition of signature wedding packages: Platinum Wedding (IDR 140M), Diamond Wedding (IDR 114M), and Wedding Blessing / Akad Nikah.
  - Streamlined corporate meeting & wedding inquiry form with automated routing to hotel sales representatives.
- **F&B & Wellness Lifestyle Exhibits**: Full photo-rich displays for Kafe Coleman (24/7 all-day dining), Symphony Lounge, Alfresco Poolside Dining, Artha Spa, and the Health Club.
- **Direct Reservation Engine Bridge**: Seamlessly integrated booking bar with date pickers, room/guest dropdowns, and promo-code validation linking directly to the hotel's central reservation engine.

### Pillar 2: Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & AISO Architecture
- **Complete Schema.org JSON-LD Suite**: Server-Side Rendered (SSR) structured data covering `Hotel`, `LodgingBusiness`, `HotelRoom`, `EventVenue`, `MeetingRoom`, `FoodEstablishment`, and `FAQPage`.
- **Global AI Factsheet Deployment**: Implementation of `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` enabling AI search bots (`GPTBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `Google-Extended`) to read and synthesize hotel data in under 10 milliseconds.
- **Instant Search Push Protocol**: Automated integration with Microsoft Bing / Copilot **IndexNow API** for immediate indexing upon publishing new room offers or banquet promotions.
- **Google Hotel Center Integration**: Setup and verification of Google **Free Booking Links** (allowing organic direct room rates to appear in Google Search and Google Maps without PPC ad spend).
- **Reputation Authority & Entity Sync**: Audit and alignment of Name, Address, Phone (NAP), and geolocation data across Google Business Profile, TripAdvisor, Apple Maps, and OTA profiles.

### Pillar 3: AI Assistant Compatibility & Integration Container
- **Pre-Configured Widget Slot**: Ready-to-plug frontend container reserved exclusively for the standalone JourneyOn AI Conversational Booking Assistant.
- **Clean Event Hooks**: Native JavaScript custom event listeners for booking actions, modal openings, and check-in date pre-fills.
- **Zero Technical Overlap**: Ensures this web infrastructure acts as the high-speed delivery platform without interfering with or replicating the separate commercial scope of conversational booking engines.
