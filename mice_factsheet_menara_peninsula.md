# MICE Factsheet & Meeting Venues - Menara Peninsula Hotel Jakarta

## 1. MICE Features Overview
- **Number of Meeting Rooms**: 15 to 20 flexible meeting and function rooms (including ballrooms and break-out rooms).
- **Scale Capability**: Suitable for board meetings, corporate training, seminars, product launches, grand exhibitions, conventions, social gatherings, and weddings.
- **Events & weddings capacity**: Up to 1,200 standing guests.
- **Support & Technology**: High-speed internet connection, state-of-the-art audiovisual (AV) equipment, sound systems, professional lighting, secretarial support from the Business Center, and customized catering options.

---

## 2. Meeting Packages & Pricing
- **Halfday Meeting Package**:
  - **Price**: **IDR 450,000** net per person, per day.
  - **Inclusions**: 1 (one) time coffee break with 3 kinds of snacks, 1 (one) time lunch or dinner, and usage of the function room for a maximum of 4 hours.
- **Fullday Meeting Package**:
  - **Price**: **IDR 550,000** net per person, per day.
  - **Inclusions**: 2 (two) times coffee break with 3 kinds of snacks, 1 (one) time lunch or dinner, and usage of the function room for a maximum of 8 hours.
- **Additional Event Charges**: 
  - Standard food stalls, sound setups, and additional hourly usage can be quoted upon query.

## 3. Major Meeting Venues & Specifications

### A. Cengkeh Ballroom
- **Floor Area**: 735 sqm (7,911.5 sq. ft.)
- **Dimensions**: 68.9 ft x 114.8 ft (approx. 21 meters x 35 meters)
- **Ceiling Height**: 23.0 ft (approx. 7 to 8 meters)
- **Maximum Capacities**:
  - **Theater Layout**: 1,000 to 1,200 persons (standing cocktail capacity up to 1,200).
  - **Classroom Layout**: 400 persons.
  - **Banquet Rounds**: 400 persons.
  - **Cocktail Reception**: 400 (seated/cocktail rounds setup) to 1,200 (standing).
  - **U-Shape Layout**: 150 persons.
- **Key Features**: High grand ceiling with wooden beams, column-free, ideal for grand weddings, major exhibitions, and large-scale corporate conventions. Can be partitioned into smaller rooms.

### B. Cengkeh 1
- **Floor Area**: 367 sqm (3,950.4 sq. ft.)
- **Dimensions**: 68.9 ft x 55.8 ft (approx. 21 meters x 17 meters)
- **Ceiling Height**: 23.0 ft (approx. 7 to 8 meters)
- **Maximum Capacities**:
  - **Theater Layout**: 350 persons.
  - **Classroom Layout**: 150 persons.
  - **Banquet Rounds**: 150 persons.
  - **Cocktail Reception**: 150 persons.
  - **U-Shape Layout**: 75 persons.

### C. Other Meeting Rooms (Breakouts & Mid-sized)
- **Total Rooms**: Up to 15-20 function rooms available on multiple levels (such as Lobby Level, Mezzanine Level, and Floor 5).
- **Flexibility**: Rooms are partitionable and configurable for boardroom layouts (U-Shape, Boardroom) or theatre styling, ranging from 10 guests up to 200 guests.
