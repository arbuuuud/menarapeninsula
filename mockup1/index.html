ABOUT MENARA PENINSULA

Sophisticated Comfort in The Heart of Jakarta

Standing tall as a legendary icon of 4-star hospitality on the strategic border of West and Central Jakarta, Menara Peninsula Hotel offers contemporary comfort with direct access to the Gatot Subroto business district and the Soekarno-Hatta International Airport toll road.

With 380 elegantly appointed guest rooms and suites, extensive meeting (MICE) facilities designed for both large corporate events and intimate gatherings, and fine culinary selections at our restaurant, we are fully committed to ensuring the success of your business itineraries and the comfort of your stay.
